# Binders 0.14.0

Binders works with a hub: the Binders server your team shares notes, a board and agents through, with its web app. Sign in to one, link a binder, and its notes, folders, pictures and cards stay the same on your Mac and on the hub, both ways, offline too. The team database is gone: a team shares through a hub, or a folder.

## Linking a binder to a hub

- Settings → Team signs this Mac in to a hub, in your browser.
- Link to Hub…, from a binder's menu, asks one thing: a new binder on the hub, named as this one, or one of the hub's you're in. Its notes, folders and cards go up, and are checked once there.
- From then on its notes and folders go both ways through the hub, and two people typing in one note both keep their words. Pictures go up and come down, and a note's history comes from the hub.
- A linked binder leaves to the hub what the hub does: its board is the hub's (the Overview links to it), Distill is the hub's, its cards go to the hub's agents, and it's shared through the hub. A to-do still becomes a card on its board.
- Send to Hub…, when you choose: its meetings (with their transcripts if you like), captures and knowledge, and this Mac's dictations, as notes in folders of their own. Sending again updates them.
- Send a meeting to a linked binder as a note, from the meeting.

## Moving a team to a hub

- Move to a Hub… sends a team's binders to a hub, once; a teammate's Mac that finds one moved joins it and keeps what it wrote that the hub never had.

## Fixed

- A binder whose link to the hub was lost (an older Binders opened the library) is linked back by itself when Binders starts, its cards paired with the hub's so none goes up twice.
