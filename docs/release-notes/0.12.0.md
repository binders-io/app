# Binders 0.12.0

The Manager, an agent that tidies your boards and folders, distills notes and answers what you ask it. Claude Code or a local model can be the Manager. The Agents page is now where you talk to every agent. Distill no longer adds the same work again each time it reads a note. This release is for the Mac; there's no new Binders for iPhone with it.

## The Manager

- One agent can be the Manager: one of your Claude Code agents, or a local model. Choose it under The Manager at the top of Settings → Agents. Giving it the role asks you first, and says what it may do.
- Ask it anything about your binders ("what's blocked and why?") or ask it to do something ("tidy the Personal board", "distill the roadmap note"), on the Agents page. Tidy asks it to tidy one binder or every binder. A tidy every day or every week is off until you choose it, and runs only while your Mac is plugged in, cool and idle.
- It works with Binders' tools alone: it reads your binders, notes, meetings and boards the way the tools you set up do, edits, merges and closes cards, makes, renames and fills folders, and distills notes. It leaves alone the cards an agent holds or that wait on you, and a team's boards.
- It never changes a note itself: it suggests an edit, which you review line by line and accept or dismiss. Accepted, it's your edit, and the earlier version stays in the note's history.
- It can't delete anything or start another agent. Each request lists what it changed, and Undo Its Changes puts it all back at once.
- A Claude Code Manager runs in an empty folder of its own, with its permission checks on, and no files, commands or web.
- A local model as the Manager: Settings → Agents → Add a Local Model as the Manager…. It's the model Distill uses in Settings → AI, or another you name on the same server (Ollama, a model in Binders, or your OpenAI-compatible server). Binders runs it one step at a time, with the same tools, limits and Undo, and it takes no cards from your boards. A bigger model follows the steps better.

## Talk to your agents

- The Agents page in the sidebar is where you talk to your agents. On the left are the Manager and each agent, saying what it's doing: working on a card, a card that needs you, idle or off. Under the one you pick are its conversations, with the finished ones under Earlier.
- Pick an agent to see what it holds and what's meant for it, and write to it. Your message becomes a card meant for it, in Ready, on a board it takes cards from, and Binders hands it over as Give to does. Its first line is the card's title.
- Pick a conversation to read and answer it as the card's own window shows it: the agent's replies and questions, what waits on you, and the box to answer. Open Card opens the card in its window.
- The Manager has its own conversation: your requests and its replies, with Stop, Undo and Reply, and the note edits it suggests.

## Distill

- Distill no longer adds the same work again each time it reads a note. An item it finds is left out when the note already has it, in any section, even in other words: a title that says the same thing, or one with the same description or the same quote from the note. "Write tests for login" and "Write tests for signup" stay two items.
- Items 0.11.0 held back from a long part of a note are offered once, not on every reading.
- The repeats already waiting in your notes' Distill lists are cleared the first time 0.12.0 opens: the first of each stays, cards already on your boards are kept, and the repeats count as dismissed, so they're never proposed again.
- A long note's parts keep every item they found.
