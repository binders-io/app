# Binders 0.4.1

Joining a team space finds it for you.

- Join team space now finds the team space near the folder you pick: the shared folder itself, a folder inside it such as Notes, or the drive it sits in, like the top of your OneDrive.
- When it can't, it says what to do. A folder shared with you only reaches your Mac once you add it to your own drive (in OneDrive, Add shortcut to My files), and if only the team's `_binders` folder was shared, it says to share and add the whole team folder instead.
- Creating a team space at the top of a cloud drive, which can't be shared as a whole, is refused with an explanation.
- The help page and the team guide cover sharing step by step, and how Binders tells teammates apart without accounts.
