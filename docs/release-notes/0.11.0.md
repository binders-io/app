# Binders 0.11.0

Distill, faster and beside the note: a Distill button in every note's toolbar, the work items in a list beside the note as the model finds them, and Distill reading what you change in the background while you write. Mermaid diagrams are drawn in your notes, Antigravity joins the AI tools and board agents, and a note's cards show in the note at the words they came from, and together on the board. This release is for the Mac; there's no new Binders for iPhone with it.

## Distill

- Faster. Distill now asks your model for short answers, each item's title, the lines of the note it came from, who it's for and its size, so the list fills sooner. The brief and what done looks like are written once you place an item, card by card in the background, agents' cards first. Until then the card shows the note's lines it came from, and an agent's card waits in Ready, saying "Writing the brief for …", and goes to the agent once its brief is in. Your own cards and your teammates' are theirs at once, and a description you edit yourself is never written over.
- Distill again reads only what changed. Click Distill on a note it has read, and only the sections changed since are read again; when nothing changed, the model isn't asked at all and the button says Up to date. Distill Again, in the list's … menu, reads the whole note again.
- The parts of a long note no longer wait for each other, and with Ollama or a server two parts are read at once. With the model built into Binders, what every part's question shares is read once and kept, and the Distill model loads in the background when you open a note with Distill on, so it's ready when you click, unless you're dictating or recording, or it wouldn't fit beside the models already loaded.
- How fast Distill reads depends on the model you choose for it. A larger model finds more of the work and says better who each item is for, but reads more slowly. Settings → AI → Distill lets you pick a model for Distill alone, and when a reading took 3 minutes or more, the Work bar says so and offers a quicker model you have installed.
- The Distill button. Every note's toolbar has one. The first click on a note never distilled turns Distill on and starts reading: there's no separate step. Note → Distill, ⇧⌘D, does the same for the note in front, and you can change it in Settings → Shortcuts. The button says what's happening: "Distilling" with a count while it reads, with × to cancel under the pointer; "3 new" when a reading in the background found items; and Up to date when nothing changed.
- The Distill list sits beside the note, as a tab next to Work and Digest, or over the note's right side when there's no room. Items appear while the model reads, in the note's order, each with who it's for in their colour, its size and its section. Click the list, then ↑ and ↓ to move, Return to accept (the item goes on the note's board, with Undo), Delete to dismiss (Distill Again never proposes it again), Space to edit, ⌘Return to accept every row left, and ⌘Z to undo. Selecting a row lights its lines in the note, and clicking a lit line selects its row. Distill Again's offers to update a card's brief or close a card are rows too.
- Watch it read. While a reading you started runs, a band of light sweeps down the part being read, and each item found lifts out of its lines and flies into the list, or into the Distill button when the list is closed. With Reduce Motion on, the part being read is lit and nothing moves.
- Distill in the background. For a note with Distill on, a little after you stop typing, Distill reads the sections you changed and adds what it finds to the list, counted on the button and the Work bar. It never opens a sheet, alerts or notifies you, and nothing goes on the board until you accept it. It waits while you dictate, record or have All-day capture on, on battery below 20%, in Low Power Mode or when your Mac is hot. Turn it off in Settings → AI → Distill, with Distill in the background while I write.
- Distill picks who each item is for from the people and agents who can do it. It's told the names of the binder's agents, with what each is for, you, and your teammates. It names an agent when what it's for covers the work, even when you approve it at the end, such as a post it drafts and asks you about, and you only for what only a person can do, such as a decision, a call or a payment. Give each agent a What it's for in Settings → Agents so Distill can pick it. When it names no one, your decision model suggests someone a moment later, reading the item's lines as well as its title.
- The review sheet no longer opens after a reading, and Review on the Work bar opens the list. The sheet is still there for this release, as Review All… in the list's … menu: it says who picked who each item is for, and Suggest Who Again asks your decision model again for items meant for no one yet.
- Long notes. A slow model is no longer stopped after five minutes: Distill waits as long as the model keeps writing, and stops a part only when the model hasn't started within five minutes or stops writing for 90 seconds. A part cut short keeps the items it finished, the Work bar says which part it was, and the parts after it are still read. The list says "Reading part 2 of 5 · 9 items so far", and Cancel keeps what was found. Distill measures how fast your model writes, and sizes the parts of a long note so each takes about two minutes.
- Turn Off Distill for This Note…, in the note's … menu, is the new name for Stop Distilling….

## Mermaid diagrams in notes

- A fenced block that starts with ```` ```mermaid ```` is drawn as its diagram in the note when your cursor is outside it. Click the diagram to edit its code, with the drawing under it, drawn again a moment after you stop typing. Right-click it for Edit Diagram and Copy Image.
- A diagram Mermaid can't read shows its code, with one line saying what's wrong.
- Diagrams are drawn on your Mac, with nothing loaded from the web, and follow light and dark mode. The note's Markdown stays as you wrote it: copying, search, agents and your team get the code, and Binders for iPhone shows it as text.

## Antigravity

- Google retired Gemini CLI for personal accounts, and Antigravity takes its place. Settings → MCP adds Binders to Antigravity with one click, in `~/.gemini/config/mcp_config.json`, which its command-line tool and its app share, and Remove takes it out again. Gemini CLI's row is marked as retired for personal accounts, and shows only where Gemini CLI is already set up.
- Antigravity can be a board agent: Settings → Agents → Add Agent → Antigravity in a Repository…, when `agy` is on your Mac. Before its first run, add `"mcp(binders/*)"` to `permissions.allow` in `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/settings.json` yourself, as Antigravity refuses any tool no rule allows when no one is there to approve. A run edits files in the card's own worktree, runs only the commands your Antigravity settings allow, inside its sandbox, reaches nothing on the network, and uses Binders' tools for that card only. A command it wasn't allowed is named on the card. It can't be set to Plans only, and Binders won't start it when your settings would let it act outside its worktree without asking. The card's next run carries on the same conversation, and the card gives the command to look in.
- Writing capture can keep the prompts you send Antigravity: Settings → Writing capture → In the terminal has Copy Hook, for you to add to `~/.gemini/config/hooks.json` yourself.

## Work in the note, and a note's cards on the board

- A note shows where its work went. The words each card came from are tinted in the colour of the card's state, with a chip at the end of the line saying where the card is, such as "In progress · Claude Code" or "Done", and each heading counts its section's cards. Hover a chip for each card's board, column, who has it and its last update, and click it to open the card. The marks are drawn, never written into the note. Show Work in the Note, on the Work bar, turns them off. On a card, Distilled from quotes the words it came from and opens the note there.
- Group by Note, a switch in the board's header, shows each note's cards together in every column, under the note's title with how its work is going ("1 of 4 done"). Drop a card on a group's header to put it there. A card you made by hand can join a note's group with Part of… on its Details.
- Choose several cards: ⌘-click, ⇧-click for a run in a column, ⌘A for every card, or Select All on a group's header. A bar over the columns then moves, assigns, deletes or groups them all, and one Undo puts it back. A card an agent or a teammate is working on stays where it is, and the bar says so. Assigning to an agent asks once, then hands each card over as Give to does, with a receipt on each. Select on Board, in a note's Work panel, chooses that note's cards on its board.
- Put on Board is tracked. A card put on the board from a note counts as that note's work, as Distill's cards do, even with Distill off: the note shows it, the Work bar and Work panel follow it, and Group by Note keeps it with the note. Select words in a note and Control-click to put just them on the board, or choose Put Each Line on Board for a card per line. Note → Put on Board, ⇧⌘B, puts the selection, or the whole note when nothing is selected. Cards put on the board before this count as their note's too.

## Smaller changes

- The board and its cards have a new look.
- Text fields in Settings and the sheets it opens have their name above a full-width box, written from the left, and long text grows to a few lines as you type.
- Settings → Privacy keeps Open data folder, Acknowledgements and Delete all history readable at the window's smallest width.

## For developers: MCP and automations

- `propose_work_items` takes a `quote` for each item, the note's own words it came from, and `for` may name one of the user's teammates as well as an agent. `distill_note`'s items, and `distilled_from` in `list_tasks` and `get_task`, may carry `quote`.
- `list_tasks` and `get_task` give `note_id` and `note_title` for a card in a note's group, whether Distill made it, it was put in by hand, or it was put on the board from the note. `distilled_from` stays only on cards Distill made. `note_progress` covers every note with cards on the board, not only notes with Distill on. A card put on the board from a note gives an agent no way into the note: `get_note_history` and `get_note_version` refuse its id.
- Antigravity's runs get Binders' server in a `.agents/mcp_config.json` in the card's worktree, with the board tools for that card only, which Binders takes out again before it commits.

## Fixes

- Fixed: Distill stopped long notes with "The model took too long to answer" after five minutes and lost everything the model had written, even while it was still writing. A reading now goes on while the model writes, and a part that stops keeps its finished items.
- Fixed: an error on the first part of a long note ended the whole reading. The other parts are read now, and the Work bar says which part failed.
