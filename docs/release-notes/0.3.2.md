# Binders 0.3.2

Spoken corrections work with every model.

- "Send it by Friday, actually make that Thursday afternoon" now comes out as "send it by Thursday afternoon", even on the smallest model and with AI formatting off. When you correct a day, a time, a number or a month with "actually", "make that", "no wait", "sorry, I meant", "I mean" or "or rather", Binders settles it before the model sees your words.
- The model also gets examples for other corrections ("send it to Sam, sorry, I meant Sarah" becomes "send it to Sarah"), and no longer treats a new sentence that starts with "Actually" as a correction.
