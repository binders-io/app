# Binders 0.4.2

Teammates' changes show up as soon as they arrive, and their to-dos are theirs.

- Binders now watches the team folder and syncs a second after your drive brings in a teammate's meeting or note, instead of checking every 30 seconds. The 30-second check stays as a fallback.
- In a teammate's meeting notes, "You" meant them but read as you: their action items showed up as yours. Their "You" now shows as their name, and an item with your name on it shows as yours, in the notes, the to-do lists, the board's Add To-dos, and for AI tools over MCP.
