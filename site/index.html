Why Binders

Most tools remember a fragment.
Binders remembers the work.

Your work is already spread across calls, notes, chats, and the files on your Mac. Binders gives it one private shape: what you said, heard, wrote, promised, and finished. Ask it a question, hand a piece of work to an agent, or open the board and see what’s next.

01

Capture in the moment

Dictation, meeting notes, writing capture, and all-day capture collect the work as it happens, without asking you to switch apps.

02

Keep the connections

People, projects, decisions, and sources stay linked, so a question can travel from a name to everything they were part of.

03

Put it to work

Promises become to-dos, notes become cards, and a board lets you and your agents move the work forward together.

Privacy

Private by construction,
not by policy.

Binders has no account, no analytics, and no cloud of ours. The speech models and the language model run on your Mac; the files sit in a folder you can open. Here is exactly what stays, and what leaves only when you point it somewhere.

Read the privacy notes Read the code

Open source · GPLv3

Stays on this Mac

  • Audio, transcripts, notes, meetings, and captured writing, in one folder you can open.
  • Speech recognition with Parakeet or Whisper on the Neural Engine.
  • The search index and the knowledge graph.
  • Your dictionary, snippets, and settings.

Leaves only where you point it

  • Text sent to your language model: on your Mac by default, or a server you choose.
  • Binders you share with your team, through your own cloud folder.
  • A web hook you set up, to the address you give it.
  • AI tools and agents you connect: what you let them read, or the card you hand them.
  • A one-time download of the speech models, and of the built-in model if you use it.
