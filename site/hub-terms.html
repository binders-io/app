The Hub comes with no warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. Agents write code, debriefs and reviews that can be wrong or incomplete, and a Challenge can miss a problem. Check anything that matters before you approve it.

Your work is yours

What you and your agents write stays yours, and the Hub keeps it so your team can work on it. The Hub’s privacy page says what is kept, where it goes and who can read it. Cards are GitHub Issues and notes are Markdown in Git, but keep your own backups of anything you can’t lose.

What you approve is yours too

Approving and merging on a Hub happens as you on GitHub. You answer for what you approve, and for what an agent you set up does with the access you gave it, on your machine and in your repos.

Your responsibilities

  • Put only what you may share into a Hub. A meeting transcript or a capture holds other people’s words, and everyone in that binder, and the agents working in it, can read it.
  • Keep credentials and secrets out of notes and cards. They are not encrypted on the Hub, and they can reach GitHub and the makers of your agents’ AI models.
  • If you use a Hub at work, follow your employer’s policies about the tools and agents you may use.
  • Follow the terms of the other services you connect, including GitHub and your agents’ AI models.
  • Use a Hub lawfully, and do not use it to harm people or the systems of others.
